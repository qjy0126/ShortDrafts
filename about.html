About ShortDrafts

Practical writing packs for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Reels—written for people who still shoot their own clips, not AI video studios.

ShortDrafts is a small writing project, not a production company. It exists to turn one idea into words you can actually say, type on screen, and paste under a post. If something is broken, use the contact page. The name is short plus drafts: the writing you finish before you shoot.

What you’ll find here

  • The generator — one topic in; ideas, hooks, a timed script, titles, a caption, a CTA, and on-screen text out.
  • Faceless or on camera — voiceover drafts for B-roll, or talking-head lines if you want to show your face.
  • Platform copy — titles, captions, and hashtags tuned for Shorts, TikTok, or Reels.
  • Guides in FAQ — short notes on what a pack is, what it isn’t, and how the free daily cap works.

What this site will not do

ShortDrafts will not render a finished MP4, clone a voice, or lock you into avatars and stock footage. It will not promise that a pack “goes viral.” If you need a video model or an editor, copy the words and use those tools after. If a claim would need a lawyer or a lab, it does not belong in a 30-second script here.

Corrections

If you find a broken generate run, a misleading line, or a typo, email contact@shortdrafts.com or write through the contact page. Fixes to the product come first.

Advertising

This site does not run ads and does not sell a paid plan. Hosting is covered by a free daily limit of 10 packs. Details on what we store are in the privacy policy.
