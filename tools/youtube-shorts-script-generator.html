YouTube Shorts script generator

A 15–60 second spoken draft, plus hooks, titles, captions, and on-screen text. Free. No login. You still shoot the Short.

A YouTube Shorts script generator should write words you can say in one take, not a five-minute YouTube essay. ShortDrafts takes one topic and returns a pack: three angles, five hooks for the first three seconds, one timed script, five titles for the Shorts shelf, a caption, a CTA, and short 9:16 lines.

It does not export an MP4, pick B-roll, or run an avatar. That is the point. The writing step stays separate so you keep the camera.

What you get in a Shorts pack

  • Ideas ranked for a vertical clip, not a long-form outline
  • Hooks that open on a claim or a picture — not “hey guys”
  • One spoken script matched to 15–30s, 30–45s, or 45–60s
  • Titles written for search and the Shorts shelf
  • Caption, CTA, and on-screen text that sit above the interface chrome

How to generate a Shorts script

Open Write, leave the platform on YouTube Shorts, pick a style (explainer, story, listicle, funny, or faceless), and type a concrete topic. “Why cold brew tastes bitter after day two” works. “Make me go viral” does not.

Read the script out loud. If you cannot say it in the length you picked, cut before you record. Copy a title and caption into YouTube when the clip is ready.

FAQ

How long should a YouTube Shorts script be?

Pick 15–30s for a list, 30–45s for one proof, 45–60s if the recap needs a beat. YouTube allows longer Shorts; this generator stays in the minute most people actually watch.

Does this make a finished YouTube Short?

No. You get the writing layer. Record on your phone or drop the script into an editor you already use.

Can I retune the pack for TikTok or Reels?

Yes. Change the platform chip before you generate so titles and captions match the app you will post in.
