Hook generator for Shorts, TikTok, and Reels

Five openings built for the first three seconds. Not a slow intro. The same click also writes the script those hooks have to pay off.

A hook generator is only useful if the next line still makes sense. ShortDrafts does not spit isolated clickbait. Every pack leads with five hooks, then a timed script, titles, and captions so you can test more than one cold open on the same idea.

FAQ

What is a hook generator for Shorts and TikTok?

It writes several first lines meant to stop a scroll. Here you also get the script, so the open and the payoff stay in the same pack.

How long should a hook be?

Under 16 words. If you cannot say it in one breath, it is a paragraph, not a hook.

What if a hook oversells the script?

Delete it. Keep the script. A cold open that lies trains the next swipe.
