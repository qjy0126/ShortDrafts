Use case

Podcast clip scripts

Turn a long idea into a tight 20-second cut-down someone can watch without the full episode.

By Chris Lang · Published Sep 7, 2026

Cut-downs, not the episode

A feed clip has to stand alone. Paste the point you want clipped — a take, a story beat, a disagreement — not the whole transcript. The pack writes a 15–30s script a stranger can watch without knowing the show.

If you paste 2,000 words of transcript, pick the one paragraph that is the clip. The generator is not an editor of the episode file.

Two ways to use it

Talking head: sit down and say the new short script to camera, even if the original was a two-person chat. Faceless: voice the script over a waveform, B-roll, or a quote card.

  • Length: 15–30s unless the take needs a second of air
  • On-screen text for the line you want burned in before the CTA
  • Caption should name the show and the episode if you want the click-through

Keep it honest

Do not ask the generator to invent a guest quote. Bring the real line, then let the pack write the wrap around it. If the original take was messy, that is fine — the Short can be clean without becoming a different claim.

What the platforms actually say

A feed clip is not the episode. YouTube’s longer Shorts limit is useful when a take needs a second of air, not when you try to recap a 40-minute interview. Paste the one paragraph that is the clip, then let the pack write a standalone 15–30 second script.

Do not invent a guest quote. If the original line is messy, the Short can be cleaner without becoming a different claim.
