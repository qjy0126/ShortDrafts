Use case

Story and listicle Shorts

Reddit-style stories, ranked lists, and fun-fact scripts with a hard open — written so the first three seconds do the job.

By Harper Quinn · Published Sep 7, 2026

What works in 30 seconds

A story Short needs a cold open, one turn, and a landing. If the twist arrives after second 20, the setup is too long.

A listicle needs a ranked promise in the first line, then short beats. It is not a blog outline you would skip. Three items is usually enough. Five is a stretch unless each beat is one breath.

Prompt it like a brief

Paste the premise, not a vibe. “Three mistakes new landlords make with first-time tenants” beats “make a viral list.” “My roommate moved out and left the lease in my name” beats “tell a story.”

  • Style: Story or Listicle
  • Length: 15–30s for a list, 30–45s for a story with a turn
  • Pick one hook from the pack. Do not stack all five in the same clip

After you generate

Read the script out loud. Cut any line that explains the joke. Keep the on-screen text for the ranked numbers or the one sentence that makes someone stay.

If you are retelling a real incident, do not ask the generator to invent facts. Bring the true beat, then let the pack write the wrap.

What the platforms actually say

YouTube now allows Shorts up to three minutes, which tempts people to write a cold open that takes twenty seconds. That extra room is for a turn and a landing, not a greeting. If the story can be told in 30 seconds, leave the rest unshot.

YouTube even offers in-app feedback on hook and pacing. Use that after you shoot, not as a reason to skip writing the first line. The pack’s five hooks exist so you can pick one claim before you open the camera.
