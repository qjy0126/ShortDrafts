How to turn a pack into a Short

Generate, pick one angle, shoot or voice it, then paste the title and caption.

By Nina Volk · Published Sep 7, 2026

Before you generate

Come in with one topic you can show. “Morning routine for nurses” works. “Make me famous” does not.

The pass

  • Open generate and enter the topic
  • Pick Shorts, TikTok, or Reels
  • Choose talking-head or faceless, then a length
  • Generate, then pick one idea and one hook — not all of them

Then leave this site

Shoot, voice, or edit in the tools you already use. Come back only if you need another angle or a retune for a second platform.

If a generate run fails, use the contact page and include the topic and the time.

What the platforms actually say

YouTube’s create flow is record or upload, then add a title of up to 100 characters, then publish. ShortDrafts fills the writing you need before that flow: topic, platform, style, length, then a pack you can speak and paste.
