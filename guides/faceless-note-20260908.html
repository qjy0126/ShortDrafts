How to Brief the Faceless Channel Generator

Learn to prompt ShortDrafts for daily faceless content without revealing your identity.

By Maya Ellison · Published Sep 8, 2026

What channels qualify?

Faceless channels let you post daily without showing your face. Think voiceover shorts, product demos, or text-driven narratives.

ShortDrafts generates batch ideas, hooks, and timed scripts tailored to these formats.

  • Voiceover narration over B-roll
  • Product unboxing or demonstration
  • Text-on-screen storytelling

How to write an effective brief?

Start with your niche and target audience. Specify video length, tone, and any recurring motifs.

Add constraints like ‘no face shown’ and ‘focus on hands or objects’ to keep the generator on track.

  • Niche: e.g., tech gadgets, cozy gaming
  • Length: 15–30 seconds
  • Tone: casual, informative, or dramatic

Where to generate and export?

Open the ShortDrafts interface and paste your brief. The tool returns a complete package ready for editing.

To start a new project, go to the generate page in plain words—just type the URL ending in generate.html into your browser.

What the platforms actually say

A faceless brief should name what the viewer will see if you are not on camera: hands, a product, a screen, B-roll you already have rights to. YouTube still expects you to record or upload a Short and add a title. The generator only writes the words for that upload.

Keep ‘no face’ as a constraint in the topic box. Vague prompts like ‘faceless empire’ produce generic hooks. Concrete niches produce scripts you can batch.
