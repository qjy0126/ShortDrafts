Platforms

Which platforms does it support?

YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels — same idea, copy tuned to the app you post in.

By Priya Nair · Published Sep 7, 2026

What changes per platform

The idea can stay the same. Titles, captions, and hashtags shift so they sound native to the app you pick, not copied from another feed.

What does not change

A pack is still a writing pack. You still shoot or assemble the clip. Algorithm tricks, bought views, and “guaranteed viral” language are not in scope.

  • YouTube Shorts
  • TikTok
  • Instagram Reels

If you post in more than one place

Generate once per platform if you want the caption and hashtags retuned. Do not treat one caption as interchangeable across all three.

What the platforms actually say

YouTube asks for a Short title of up to 100 characters. TikTok can auto-generate captions from speech so deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers can follow the clip. Those are different jobs. One spoken script can travel; the title, caption, and on-screen lines should be retuned per app.

Generate once for the app you will post first. If you upload the same cut elsewhere, run the pack again with that platform selected instead of pasting the same caption three times.
