Is it free?

Yes. Ten packs a day, no login, no credit card, no paid plan behind the button.

By Casey Brooks · Published Sep 7, 2026

What “free” means

The generator is free to use. There is no Creator or Pro tier, and this site does not run ads on these pages.

The daily cap

You get 10 content packs per day, counted by IP address. Regenerating a pack counts as a run. The cap exists so hosting stays up, not to upsell a paid plan.

  • No account required
  • No credit card
  • The limit resets on the calendar day

If you hit the wall

Wait for the next day, or email contact@shortdrafts.com if a run failed and you think the count is wrong. Include the topic and about what time it happened.

What the platforms actually say

The 10-pack cap is a hosting limit on this site, not a YouTube rule. YouTube counts Shorts views when a clip starts to play or replay, with no minimum watch time. That metric is about their feed, not about how many drafts you generate here.

If you hit the daily wall, wait until tomorrow. Email contact@shortdrafts.com only if a run failed and the count looks wrong.

Sources
