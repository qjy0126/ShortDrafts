Can I make faceless Shorts with this?

Yes. Pick the faceless style for a voiceover draft and lines that work without your face.

By Sam Ortega · Published Sep 7, 2026

What faceless means here

You stay off camera. The pack is written to be heard and read: a voiceover script, on-screen text, and titles that still stop a thumb without a talking head.

How to generate one

On the generate page, set style to faceless. Keep the topic concrete — a product, a niche, or a single claim you can show with B-roll.

  • Read the script into a mic, or drop it into a voice tool you already use
  • Lay the on-screen lines over screen recordings, hands, or stock you have rights to
  • Use a title and caption tuned to Shorts, TikTok, or Reels

What still has to be yours

Footage, voice, and the edit. The pack does not pick clips or license music. It only writes the words so the shoot has a spine.

What the platforms actually say

Faceless still means a real clip: voice plus picture. YouTube Shorts are uploaded videos; TikTok can auto-caption speech so the words stay readable if the sound is off. Write the voiceover first, then lay picture, then check captions against the script so they match what you said.

The pack will not license B-roll or clone a voice. Bring footage and a mic you already have.
