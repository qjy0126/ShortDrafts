Do I get a finished YouTube Short video?

No. You get the writing layer. The camera, the cut, and the post are still yours.

By Riley Chen · Published Sep 7, 2026

What you walk away with

A pack of copy. You can read the script into a mic, put the on-screen lines over B-roll, and paste a title and caption when you upload.

What this site will not do

It will not stitch stock footage, clone a voice, or export a file you can upload as-is. If you need a video model or an editor, copy the words and use those tools after.

  • No finished MP4
  • No avatar or fake studio
  • No promise that a pack goes viral

A clean stop line

If a claim would need a lawyer, a lab, or a licensed trade, it does not belong in a 30-second script here. The generator writes words. You decide what is safe to say on camera.

What the platforms actually say

Creating a Short, in YouTube’s own wording, means recording or uploading a vertical clip, then adding a title and publishing. ShortDrafts does not do those last steps. It writes the spoken and on-screen copy you take into that flow.

Use an editor or a voice tool after you copy the pack. Do not wait here for an export button. There is not one.
