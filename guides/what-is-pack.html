What is an AI Shorts content generator?

One topic in. Ideas, hooks, a spoken script, titles, and captions out.

By Jordan Hale · Published Sep 7, 2026

The short answer

It is a writing tool. You type a topic, pick a platform, a style, and a length, and it returns a content pack you can speak, type on screen, and paste under a post.

It is not a video renderer. There is no MP4 at the end of the run.

What is in a pack

A pack is the words around one idea, written for a 15–60 second clip.

  • A few distinct angles so you can pick one
  • Hooks written for the first three seconds
  • A timed spoken script
  • Title options, a caption, a CTA, and on-screen lines

Who it is for

People who still shoot their own clips — talking head, B-roll, or a faceless voiceover. If you wanted an avatar studio, this is the wrong tab.

What the platforms actually say

YouTube’s help center describes Shorts as short-form videos you create on a phone, then title and upload. ShortDrafts sits one step before that: the ideas, hooks, spoken script, titles, and captions. There is no MP4 at the end of a generate run, because the shoot is still yours.

If a tool promises a finished clip from one prompt, that is a different product. This one is the writing layer so you can open the YouTube, TikTok, or Reels camera with a script in hand.
