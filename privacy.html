Privacy Policy

Last updated September 7, 2026. This explains what we collect when you generate a Shorts pack.

What we collect

When you generate a pack, the topic, platform, style, and length you submit are sent to our server, then to the language-model provider that writes the pack. We also store a daily generation count against your IP address so the free limit can be enforced.

What we do not collect

The generator does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, payment details, or social logins.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses only what the browser needs to load pages. We do not run advertising cookies on these pages.

Model providers

Generation is fulfilled by a third-party API. Do not paste passwords, private keys, or personal data you would not want processed by a writing model.

Contact

Privacy questions: contact@shortdrafts.com.
